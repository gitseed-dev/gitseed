# GitSeed Trademark Policy

GitSeed's source code is open source under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](./LICENSE).
**The license covers the code, not the brand.** "GitSeed", the GitSeed logo, and
related marks (the "Marks") are trademarks of Trent Seed. Apache-2.0 Section 6
grants no rights to use them.

The purpose of this policy is simple: anyone should be able to use, modify, and
share GitSeed freely, but users should never be confused about whether something
is the official GitSeed product.

## ✅ You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully**: "built on GitSeed", "works with GitSeed",
  "GitSeed integration for X", "a fork of GitSeed", or "compatible with GitSeed".
- **Use the name in articles, blog posts, talks, tutorials, books, and videos**
  about GitSeed.
- **Run unmodified or modified GitSeed internally** within your organization,
  including the name and logo shown in the UI.
- **Redistribute unmodified official releases** under the GitSeed name.
- **Name community resources** that are clearly non-commercial and unofficial,
  such as "GitSeed Community Discord" or "awesome-gitseed", provided you state
  that they are not affiliated with or endorsed by the project.

## ❌ You may not, without written permission

- **Name a product, fork, distribution, or company** with "GitSeed" or a
  confusingly similar name (for example, "GitSeed Pro" or "GitSeedHub").
- **Offer a hosted or managed service** using the Marks, such as
  "GitSeed Cloud", "Hosted GitSeed", or "GitSeed-as-a-Service". You *may* host
  GitSeed under your own brand and say it is "powered by GitSeed".
- **Distribute a modified version under the GitSeed name or logo.** Forks must
  rebrand; a statement like "based on GitSeed" is fine.
- **Use the Marks in a domain name, app store listing, or social media handle**
  in a way that suggests it is official.
- **Imply endorsement, partnership, or certification** by the GitSeed project.
- **Alter the logo** or use it as part of your own logo.

## Forks

Forking is encouraged; that is what open source is for. If you distribute a
modified version publicly, please:

1. Choose a different name and logo.
2. Remove or replace the GitSeed logo and name from user-facing branding
   (UI headers, app icons, package names, container image names).
3. Keep the existing `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, as Apache-2.0 requires. Keeping
   the copyright and attribution notices does **not** count as trademark misuse.

## Asking for permission

If you're unsure, or want to use the Marks for something not covered here (for
example, a conference, meetup, or commercial partnership), email
**legal@gitseed.dev** or open a
[GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/gitseed-dev/gitseed/discussions). We're
generally happy to say yes to uses that help the community.
